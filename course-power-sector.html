Power Sector Fundamentals

Explore electricity markets, regulations and the foundations of the power ecosystem.

◷ 8 hours12 modulesBeginnerSelf-paced
ABOUT THIS COURSE

A clear, structured introduction to how electricity is generated, transmitted, distributed, priced and regulated. Ideal for graduates, engineers moving into commercial roles and anyone who wants to understand the business of power.

What you’ll learn

  • How the generation → transmission → distribution chain works
  • Electricity markets, power purchase agreements and tariffs
  • The role of regulators, policy and grid operators
  • Renewables, storage and the energy transition
  • Reading key sector reports with confidence

Curriculum

  • Generation sources and plant economics
  • Transmission networks and grid operations
  • Distribution companies and consumers
  • Power purchase agreements
  • Tariff design and cost recovery
  • Power exchanges and short-term markets
  • Regulatory commissions and their role
  • Key acts and policy frameworks
  • Case studies of regulatory decisions
  • Renewable integration and storage
  • Emerging business models
  • Capstone: analyse a real sector scenario

Who is this for?

Students, working professionals and career switchers who want a practical, structured understanding of the subject — no prior experience required beyond what’s listed in the level.
